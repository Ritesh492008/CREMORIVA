## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an atmosphere of hyper-luxurious indulgence, merging the sensory artistry of artisanal haute-chocolaterie with the streamlined elegance of futuristic hospitality. Tailored for discerning gourmands, epicureans, and tech-forward luxury consumers, the interface evokes irresistible craving, exclusivity, and supreme bespoke craft.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Luxury Glassmorphism** with **Neo-Futuristic Editorial Minimalism**:
- Silky, smoked glass surfaces reminiscent of tempered obsidian and molten glass.
- Liquid golden-caramel luminescent gradients and warm hazelnut bronze framing.
- Generous micro-rhythms and high-contrast typographic pairings where aristocratic editorial serif titles meet ultra-precise geometric sans metadata.
- Polished, sensory interactions including AI Sommelier consoles, bespoke flavor profile equalizers, and seamless digital concierge touchpoints.

## Colors
The color architecture simulates decadent dessert creation: rich molten cacao, crystalline gold leaf, toasted praline, and velvet heavy cream.

- **Primary (`#E5A962`)**: Pure spun caramel gold. Used for key interactive drivers, focal actions, active sensory states, and premium highlights.
- **Secondary (`#A26C45`)**: Roasted hazelnut bronze. Provides structural authority, hairline glass bevels, and atmospheric glow boundaries.
- **Tertiary (`#F3C78E`)**: Whipped honeyed caramel. Reserved for hover lusters, soft indicator glows, and stellar ratings.
- **Neutral Dark Base (`#150B08` & `#1E120E`)**: Triple-origin espresso chocolate and deep roasted cacao nib. Forms the foundational canvases and atmospheric depth layers.
- **Velvety Tonal Accents (`#FDF9F3` & `#F5EDE4`)**: Vanilla bean silk and white chocolate ganache, delivering supreme legibility and soft, radiant contrast for text hierarchy.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the tension between classical gastronomy and modern high-tech curation:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys timeless luxury, bespoke craftsmanship, and sensory drama. Used for dramatic titles, product appellations, sensory tasting notes, and AI concierge greetings.
- **Interface & Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides crisp, modern legibility across dark glassmorphic backgrounds. Used for nutritional matrices, origin coordinates, flavor notes, price tags, and conversational prompts.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Formatted with wide letter-spacing (`0.06em` - `0.08em`) and uppercase treatments to echo fine-jewelry and perfume packaging.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:

- **Rhythm & Negative Space**: Generous margins mirror the plating presentation of Michelin-star desserts, giving 3D renders, liquid swirls, and glass panels breathing room.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: Side-by-side degustation cards, floating AI Sommelier dock, split hero with immersive chocolate liquid canvas.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 2-column sensory cards, sticky bottom ordering bar, collapsible tasting profile drawer.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: Single column with horizontal swipe carousels for artisanal gelato creations, full-screen glass drawer modals, and sticky bottom navigation.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through glowing liquid ambiance, dark smoked glass, and precise caramel light leaks rather than generic grey dropshadows:

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: Deep matte obsidian espresso (`#150B08`) layered with ambient radial glows of molten chocolate (`rgba(70, 32, 17, 0.4)`) and warm golden caramel (`rgba(229, 169, 98, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Layer (Cards & Shelves)**: Smoked cocoa glass panels (`rgba(30, 18, 14, 0.65)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` translucent bronze border (`rgba(162, 108, 69, 0.35)`).
- **Elevated Modals & Sommelier Windows**: Deepened smoked glass (`rgba(21, 11, 8, 0.88)`) surrounded by a diffuse amber halo: `box-shadow: 0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 30px rgba(229, 169, 98, 0.12)`.
- **Active / Accent Glow**: Interactive components project a warm, buttery luminance: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(229, 169, 98, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
A curvilinear, pill-dominant silhouette system captures the smooth, melted contours of gelato quenelles, drizzled chocolate ganache, and poured caramel:

- **Interactive Elements**: Buttons, selection pills, flavor tags, and search bars utilize complete pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Surface Enclosures**: Cards and glassmorphic modal containers utilize generous `rounded-xl` (2rem to 2.5rem) radii to soften dark edges into organic, confectionery forms.
- **Accents**: Circular avatar frames for AI bots and culinary masters, nestled within layered concentric bronze rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Gilded Caramel)**: Solid rich caramel gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F3C78E 0%, #E5A962 60%, #C4853A 100%)`) with deep espresso typography (`#150B08`, bold). Gentle outer glow on hover; transforms with a silky spring lift (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Secondary (Smoked Glass & Bronze)**: Semi-transparent backdrop (`rgba(30, 18, 14, 0.6)`) with `1.5px` border in hazelnut bronze (`#A26C45`) and white chocolate ganache text (`#FDF9F3`).
- **Ghost Action**: Pill-shaped text button in `#F3C78E` with a subtle underglow line that expands on hover.

### Cards & Dessert Showcase Tiles
- **Artisanal Gelato & Dessert Tiles**: High-translucency smoked chocolate cards featuring high-resolution 3D dessert splashes that break beyond the top card edge.
- Inner card details display tasting notes, origin coordinates (e.g., *72% Single-Origin Madagascar Dark Ganache*), and dynamic gold-tinted price pills.
- Subtle inner specular edge highlight on cards: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Pill-shaped micro badges displaying awards, temperature cues, or flavor characteristics (*"Velvet Espresso Crunch"*, *"Limited Batch"*, *"Smoked Salt Praline"*).
- Styled with dark cocoa backdrops, 1px warm bronze borders, and caramel metallic typography.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Glassmorphic inputs with deep espresso tinting, inset shadow, and cream placeholder text.
- Focus state activates an illuminated caramel perimeter border (`#E5A962`) with a soft outward amber diffusion (`0 0 12px rgba(229, 169, 98, 0.25)`).

### AI Dessert Sommelier & Interactive Sensory Sliders
- **Conversational Sommelier Interface**: Dark translucent floating capsule with an oscillating golden caramel aura representing voice and taste synthesis.
- **Sensory Flavor Sliders**: Bespoke multi-axis sliders (Bitterness, Sweetness, Richness, Crunch) with golden liquid track fills and pearl-caramel drag handles that leave a fading golden particle wake.

### Real-Time Ordering & Confection Modals
- Sheet drawers framed with soft hazelnut metallic bevels, featuring an interactive layer-by-layer dessert customization visualizer (toppings, infusion drops, and warm drip choices).
